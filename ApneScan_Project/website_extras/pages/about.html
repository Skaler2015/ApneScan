About ApneScan

ApneScan is a free, open-source document scanner for Windows, built with a Hindi + English interface for homes, shops, offices, cyber cafés / CSC centers, schools and clinics across India.

It turns any network (eSCL), TWAIN or WIA scanner — or even a webcam — into a fast scan-to-PDF tool with OCR, a full PDF toolkit, smart auto-naming, a built-in file manager and instant WhatsApp/Email sharing. Everything runs locally; nothing ever leaves your PC.

Why it's free

ApneScan is made to help people who scan a lot of paper every day but shouldn't have to pay for basic tools or watch ads. The code is open-source on GitHub.
