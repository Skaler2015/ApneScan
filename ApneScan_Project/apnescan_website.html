Help

Common questions

Scanner not showing?

Open Settings → Scan method and try both TWAIN and WIA. Make sure your scanner's driver is installed and the device is on.

Want faster scans?

In your profile, set DPI to 200 and colour mode to Black & White for text documents.

Is my data safe?

Yes. ApneScan runs fully on your PC. It has no ads, no tracking, and never uploads your files.
