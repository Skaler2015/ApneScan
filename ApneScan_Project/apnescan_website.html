What is ApneScan? (in short)

ApneScan is a 100% free, open-source document scanner for Windows 10 and 11 with a Hindi + English interface. It scans paper to a searchable PDF (Hindi + English OCR) from network scanners (eSCL — no drivers), USB scanners (TWAIN/WIA) with ADF duplex, a webcam, or a phone photo — then compresses, shares on WhatsApp/Email, and edits PDFs. Everything runs offline on your PC — no ads, no tracking, no uploads.

  • Price: Free (no ads, no account, no watermark)
  • Platform: Windows 10 / 11 · Languages: Hindi + English (OCR both)
  • Best for: shops, offices, clinics/hospitals, schools, CSC centres in India that scan and upload/WhatsApp PDFs
  • Alternative to: NAPS2, CamScanner (PC) — with a Hindi-first UI and government-portal PDF size targets
  • Download: apnescan.apnesoft.com · Source: GitHub

FAQ · Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Is ApneScan really free?

Yes — 100% free, no ads, no hidden charges. None of your documents ever leave your PC.

Which scanners are supported?

Network (WiFi/LAN) scanners work without drivers via eSCL — HP, Canon, Epson, Brother. USB scanners via TWAIN/WIA, with ADF duplex support.

Does it do Hindi OCR?

Yes — it creates searchable PDFs with both English and Hindi text that you can search and copy.

Can it compress PDFs?

Yes — shrink any PDF to 200KB / 500KB / 1MB / 2MB targets, perfect for portals with upload size limits.

Phone photo to PDF?

Yes — import a phone photo of a document; shadows are removed and it becomes a clean, scan-like PDF.

Share on WhatsApp?

Yes — File → Share sends a saved PDF straight to WhatsApp or Email.

Why the SmartScreen warning?

Windows shows it for new unsigned apps — click "More info → Run anyway". The code is open-source on GitHub.

Scanner not showing?

Run Settings → "Find scanner" (auto-discovery), or try TWAIN/WIA scan methods. Make sure the scanner is on and on the same network.

Can I scan without a scanner?

Yes — use a webcam or USB camera, or import photos from your phone. Both are auto-cleaned into a PDF.

Does it name documents automatically?

Yes — rename a document once (F2) and the same form auto-names next time, optionally adding the bill/claim number and date.
